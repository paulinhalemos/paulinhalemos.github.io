In this paper we connect models and data to undestand ecological communities for which the compostion of species present in these communities is manipulated experimentally. We developed a statistical approach to infer species interactions using measurements of species abundances from a variety of experiments with different species compositions. We showed the applicability of our method testing it with data ranging from bacteria, phytoplankton and plant communities, resulting in good fit to the data and obtaining quantitative predictions for thecoexistence of species and their expected abundaces.
