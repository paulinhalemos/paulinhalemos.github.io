The interactions binding species together are the product of evolution but whether 
phylogeny can serve as a proxy for inferring species interactions remains a challenge.
We developed a novel framework to test whether phylogeny influences patterns of 
coexistence and abundance of species assemblages by using phylogenetic relationships 
to parameterize species interactions. We applied our statistical framework to 19 data 
sets from three Biodiversity and Ecosystem Functioning Experiments revealing the role 
of shared evolutionary history (phylogeny) in structuring experimental communities.
