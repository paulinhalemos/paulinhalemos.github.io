The connection between microevolutionary processes and macroevolutionary patterns is still a puzzle in biology. In this contribution we attacked the micro-macro gap by investigating how processes occurring at the population level, such as gene flow and the evolution of reprouctive isolation, scale up to affect diversification rates and determine structural patterns displayed by phylogenetic trees.
