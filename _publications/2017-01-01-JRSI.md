Coevolution is a major force shaping the web of life and networks of interacting 
species and the coevolutionary process varies across ecological communities. 
In this paper we investigated how coevolution at the interface between mutualism 
and antagonism was affected by different spatial configurations, modeled through 
patterns of connection between communities and their magnitude of gene flow.
