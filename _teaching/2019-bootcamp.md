Immersive bootcamp held by The University of Chicago's Biological Sciences Division.
Around 90 incoming graduate students—representing the entire incoming BSD class—attended the program.
Students learned R programming language to analyze large, real-world data sets emphasizing rigorous, 
reproducible computational and statistical methods.

Find out more on [BSD-QBio Github repo](https://github.com/StefanoAllesina/BSD-QBio5)
