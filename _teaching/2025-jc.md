Discussion of scientific papers with students from the Serrapilheira 
[Training Program in Quantitative Ecology](https://serrapilheira.org/en/training-program-in-quantitative-biology-and-ecology/)

The final part of the training program is a retreat with invited researchers from all over the world.
During the retreat, students are exposed to cutting edge research topics and have the opportunity 
to network with invited researchers. As part of their training we discussed papers from the invited speakers.

We discussed the following papers:
* Mumby, P., Chaloupka, M., Bozec, Y. M., Steneck, R., & Montero-Serra, I. (2021). 
Revisiting the evidentiary basis for ecological cascades with conservation impacts. 
Conserv Lett 15: e12847.

* Azevedo, J. A., Faurby, S., Colli, G. R., Antonelli, A., & Werneck, F. P. (2024). 
Deforestation limits evolutionary rescue under climate change in Amazonian lizards. 
Diversity and Distributions, 30(7), e13860.

* Lapeyrolerie, M., Chapman, M. S., Norman, K. E., & Boettiger, C. (2022). 
Deep reinforcement learning for conservation decisions. 
Methods in Ecology and Evolution, 13(11), 2649-2662.

* Abbott, K. C., McCann, K. S., & Gellner, G. (2020). 
Non-equilibrium dynamics and stochastic processes. 
Theoretical Ecology: concepts and applications, 40-52.

* Shaw, A. K., & Binning, S. A. (2016). 
Migratory recovery from infection as a selective pressure for the evolution of migration. 
The American Naturalist, 187(4), 491-501.

* Cardoso, A. W., Archibald, S., Bond, W. J., Coetsee, C., Forrest, M., Govender, N., ... & Staver, A. C. (2022). 
Quantifying the environmental limits to fire spread in grassy ecosystems. 
Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 119(26), e2110364119.

* Quintero, I., Lartillot, N., & Morlon, H. (2024). 
Imbalanced speciation pulses sustain the radiation of mammals. 
Science, 384(6699), 1007-1012.

* Cobbold, C. A., Lutscher, F., & Yurk, B. (2022). 
Bridging the scale gap: Predicting large-scale population dynamics from small-scale 
variation in strongly heterogeneous landscapes.
Methods in Ecology and Evolution, 13(4), 866-879.

* Carvalheiro, L. G., Veldtman, R., Shenkute, A. G., Tesfay, G. B., Pirk, C. W. W., Donaldson, J. S., & Nicolson, S. W. (2011).
Natural and within‐farmland biodiversity enhances crop productivity.
Ecology letters, 14(3), 251-259.

* Maynard, D. S., Miller, Z. R., & Allesina, S. (2020). 
Predicting coexistence in experimental ecological communities. 
Nature ecology & evolution, 4(1), 91-100.

* Gonzalez, C. G., Van Cauwelaert, E. M., Boyer, D., Perfecto, I., Vandermeer, J., & Benítez, M. (2021). 
High-order interactions maintain or enhance structural robustness of a coffee agroecosystem network. 
Ecological Complexity, 47, 100951.
