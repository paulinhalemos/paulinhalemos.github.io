TA for the "Souther Summer School on Mathematical Biology" for multiple years.
This summer school is held at the International Center for Theoretical Ecology ([ICTP](https://www.ictp-saifr.org/))
located at IFT-UNESP (Instituto de Física Teórica).
Designed for graduate students (and exceptional undergraduates) in physics, 
mathematics, ecology, and epidemiology.
