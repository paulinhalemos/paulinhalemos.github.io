I'm a theoretical ecologist interested in unraveling the rules that govern ecological communities and 
how processes at the population level scale up influencing the structure and functioning of ecological 
communities. My work involves finding exciting ways to adress ecological and evolutionary questions from
a theoretical perspective.

I am currently a REPAT-CNPq independent investigator at [The University of Sao Paulo](www.usp.br) in 
the [Ecology Department](https://www.ib.usp.br/ecologia.html). 

Previous I was a postdoctoral scholar at [The University of Chicago](www.uchicago.edu) working with 
[Dr. Stefano Allesina](https://stefanoallesina.github.io/).
I have a bachelor and teaching degree in Biology from the University of São Paulo, where I also did my 
Master's in Ecology with [Dr. Paulo Guimarães](https://guimaraeslab.weebly.com/) in the Ecology Department. 
I hold a PhD from the [State University of Campinas](https://www.unicamp.br/) where I worked with 
[Dr. Marcus Aguiar](https://sites.ifi.unicamp.br/aguiar/).
